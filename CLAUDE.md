# Apyclipy Developer Guide

## Design System
Read `DESIGN.md` before making any visual or UI changes. Follow the established Electric Obsidian and Royal Indigo palette, font pairings, spacing, and micro-interactions.

- Primary theme: **Calm Obsidian & Royal Indigo** — clean, generous whitespace, dark/light balance, restrained indigo accents.
- Avoid cluttered neon colors or uncoordinated gradient clashes.
